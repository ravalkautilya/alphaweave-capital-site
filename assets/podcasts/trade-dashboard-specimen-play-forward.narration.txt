This is the AlphaWeave play-forward trade blotter. PSM means position size multiple. The replay follows one directional sleeve, monthly A I rotatable directional. The chart starts at measured start, the P and L line moves, trades are taken from the position tracker, annotations group events by timestamp, risk-capped weights change over time, and the risk cards are populated.

We begin at February second, twenty twenty-six, at thirteen thirty. The measured window starts here, so the left side of the chart is the first accountable point. The active strategy shown for the sleeve is buy and hold. That matters because this replay is mainly showing asset switching and sizing behavior inside the sleeve, while still making the strategy state visible. The first action is a two-name entry: AAPL is bought and MSFT is bought, both with PSM point nine two. Because those trades happen at the same timestamp, the dashboard should show one grouped annotation, not two overlapping labels.

As the replay advances, the P and L line is already moving. That matters. A flat line would mean the dashboard is showing a placeholder, a missing series, or the wrong state source. Here the line is sourced from the measured portfolio series, and the risk cards below the chart are live from the same run payload. The weight panels are also part of the story: sleeve weight, strategy-in-sleeve weight, position asset weights, and risk-capped position weights are shown so the viewer can see how the portfolio composition changes.

On February third at nineteen thirty, the sleeve rotates its assets. AAPL is sold after weaker relative momentum, while NVDA is bought as the stronger risk-adjusted candidate. This is the first mixed timestamp: a sell and a buy occur together, so the annotation groups both actions in one place. The point of the grouping is readability and timestamp fidelity.

On February fifth at thirteen thirty, MSFT is resized. This should not appear as a second buy. A resize means there was already an open position, and the position tracker changed the target size. The annotation is yellow because resize is neither a clean entry nor a clean exit. The PSM shown for that resize is point seven eight.

On February sixth at nineteen thirty, COIN enters the sleeve at PSM point eight four. Economically, COIN is the higher-beta, crypto-linked security in this asset set, so adding it changes the risk character of the directional sleeve. This is an asset-switching event inside the same sleeve, not a separate sleeve. By this point the risk-capped weights have moved away from the initial AAPL and MSFT split. The dashboard should show that the current risk-capped book is no longer static.

On February tenth at thirteen thirty, MSFT is sold and AMD is bought. This is a capital rotation. One holding leaves the sleeve, and a semiconductor exposure is added. Below the chart, the risk cards remain populated: exposure, capital in use, available capital, max drawdown, annual return, and Sharpe are all non-zero.

On February eleventh at nineteen thirty, COIN is resized with PSM point seven four. This is deliberately smaller than the earlier entries: the path is positive, but volatility is higher, so the position size multiple tightens.

On February thirteenth at thirteen thirty, NVDA is resized with PSM point eight eight to respect risk-capped position weights. Later that same day, AMD is sold and AAPL is bought again at PSM point nine zero. The final state shows the risk-capped book changing across assets in the weight cards, while the blotter shows the actual position-tracker executions.

The important conclusion is simple: this dashboard is now showing the run as a play-forward record. The plot starts at measured start. The line is not flat. BUY, SELL, and RESIZE events are shown at their timestamps. Same-time actions are grouped. Strategy state is visible. Asset switching is visible. The risk-capped weights change. The risk cards are not empty. And the blotter prices come from the execution table, not from missing decision-event placeholders.
