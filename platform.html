Capital Management

Decide where capital goes, how much is deployed, and why.

AlphaWeave connects asset and strategy selection to portfolio allocation. The capital management layer translates selected opportunities into position sizes within the configured capital budget, sleeve allocations, and portfolio limits.

  1. Set the capital budget

    Start with the run's configured capital and cash reserve. These settings establish the budget for portfolio allocation.

  2. Allocate across sleeves

    A sleeve is a portion of the portfolio with its own assets and strategies. Allocation groups define how capital is distributed within the configured portfolio structure.

  3. Size each position

    Translate the allocation into position weights. A position size multiplier (PSM) can increase or reduce the proposed size according to the configured policy and decision review.

  4. Apply portfolio limits

    Check proposed exposure against position, gross and net exposure, and other configured risk constraints. Sizing recommendations remain subject to the risk management layer.

  5. Measure the outcome

    Use trade events, portfolio weights, and capital-weighted reports to follow capital changes and assess the contribution of selection, sizing, and execution.

Capital management and risk management work together. Capital management determines the proposed allocation and size; risk management constrains what the portfolio is allowed to hold. AI-assisted conviction does not override those limits.
